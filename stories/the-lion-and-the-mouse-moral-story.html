The Lion and the Mouse: A Short Story with Moral

Welcome to our moral values series. Today, we share a touching short story about kindness and friendship for kids. In this story, we learn that no act of kindness, no matter how small, is ever wasted. Let’s read
'The Lion and the Mouse'.

Once, in a deep green forest, a mighty lion was fast asleep under the cool shade of a tree. A tiny little mouse happened to run across the grass and accidentally scrambled right up the lion's nose!

The lion woke up with a loud roar! He angrily trapped the frightened mouse under his massive paw. "How dare you wake me up!" growled the lion, opening his big jaws to swallow the mouse.

"Oh, please spare me, King of the Forest!" squeaked the tiny mouse. "I did not mean to wake you. Please let me go, and I promise I will help you one day!"

The lion laughed heartily at the idea. "A tiny mouse helping a big lion? Ridiculous!" But since he was in a good mood, he lifted his paw and let the little mouse scurry away.

A few weeks later, while hunting in the jungle, the lion was caught in a hunter's strong rope net. He struggled and pulled, but the ropes were too tight. The lion roared loudly in despair, his voice echoing through the forest.


"Who do you think will hear the lion's roar?"


The tiny mouse heard the sad roar and ran quickly to the spot. Seeing the lion trapped, the mouse said, "Do not worry, my friend! I will save you." The mouse immediately began chewing through the thick ropes with his sharp little teeth.

Chomp, chomp, chomp! Soon, the mouse nibbled a big hole in the net, and the lion scrambled out, completely free. The lion realized his mistake and felt grateful. He smiled and said, "Thank you, little friend. You proved that even the smallest creature can be a big help." From that day on, the lion and the mouse were the best of friends.
